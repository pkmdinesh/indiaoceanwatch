🪸 Coral Bleaching Alert System

INCOIS CBAS · Satellite Thermal Stress Monitoring

ℹ️ What do these metrics mean?

HotSpot (HS): Instantaneous Sea Surface Temperature (SST) anomaly above the maximum monthly mean — indicates immediate thermal stress.

Degree of Heating Weeks (DHW): Thermal stress accumulated over a 12-week window (°C-weeks) — indicates bleaching severity and potential mortality risk.

ℹ️ Cyclone Intensification Energy Guide:
< 50 kJ/cm²Low / Unfavorable
50 - 80 kJ/cm²Moderate / Favorable
> 80 kJ/cm²⚠️ Rapid Intensification

Tropical Cyclone Heat Potential measures integrated upper-ocean thermal energy down to the 26°C isotherm. High TCHP (>80 kJ/cm²) prevents cyclone-induced cooling and fuels severe cyclone intensification.
